Enroll now

Founded by Mr. Edward Calloway.

Nova Institute opened in 2010.

Conversation first. Grammar in service of it — never the other way round.

Most students arrive having studied a language for years without being able to hold a conversation. We start every course with speaking, and let grammar catch up to what students already want to say.

Classes stay small — eight students at most — so every person speaks every session, not just the confident ones.

Speak from lesson one

Even absolute beginners are speaking full sentences by the end of their first class.

Small, fixed groups

The same eight people for the whole term, so the room gets comfortable fast.

Tested, not guessed

Every student starts at the right level, thanks to the placement test — not a self-assessment.
