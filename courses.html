TTC (Teacher Training Course) is a structured program designed to prepare individuals for a career in teaching. TESOL

TESOL stands for Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages. It is a professional certification and academic field that prepares teachers to instruct English learners whose native language is not English. TESOL covers both teaching English in non-English-speaking countries (like EFL — English as a Foreign Language) and in English-speaking countries to immigrants or international students (like ESL — English as a Second Language).
